Treat mold as a warning as well as a health hazard, because the hidden moisture feeding it puts your property's structural integrity at risk. Effective mold remediation goes far beyond scrubbing visible mold off surfaces. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Mold Remediation Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30 to 50%) with dehumidifiers and proper ventilation
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
  • Mold can get going after just 24 to 48 hours of dampness, so have leaks fixed within 24 hours

How Our Mold Remediation Works

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle mold remediation from start to finish:

1

Mold Inspection and Sampling

The inspection locates hidden as well as visible growth and pins down where the moisture comes from, and our certified technician may send air or surface samples out for lab analysis.

2

Before removal starts, the affected area is sealed behind polyethylene sheeting and held at negative pressure with HEPA scrubbers, stopping spores from drifting into rooms that are not affected.

Affected materials are carefully removed and disposed of following EPA guidelines. Remaining structural surfaces are treated with professional-grade antimicrobial solutions.

4

The moisture source behind the problem is corrected, any drying still needed is finished, and containment comes down only after post-remediation verification confirms the work was successful.

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Closets, bathrooms or other damp, enclosed rooms that always smell musty
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
  • Condensation forming regularly on windows, walls or cold surfaces
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
  • Mold or mildew that comes back soon after you clean it
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
