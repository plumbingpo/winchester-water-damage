A sewage backup is one of the most hazardous situations a property owner can face. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Winchester Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Winchester, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.
