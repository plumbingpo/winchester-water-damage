Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Winchester

A roof leak rarely starts where the stain appears. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Your First Steps

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.

Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Rubber boots around plumbing vent pipes that split after years in the sun.
What Is at Risk

What Happens if You Wait

Wall Cavities

Rain sometimes follows the framing into a wall, where the insulation and the hidden side of the drywall take it up. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

Roof Framing

Repeated wetting makes decking, trusses and rafters turn dark, swell up and go soft. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Insulation in the Attic

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Drying is sometimes possible with fiberglass. Soaked cellulose usually has to be removed.

Drywall Ceilings

Staining, sagging and loss of strength follow when drywall is not dried. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Coverage

Does Insurance Cover It?

When a leak happens suddenly and by accident, for example a branch going through the roof or wind stripping shingles, many homeowners policies pay for the water damage inside. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings for your adjuster, but coverage depends on your policy, so read it or ask your agent.
